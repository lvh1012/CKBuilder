# **Community Keeps Building**

## **Terms and Conditions of Participation**

**CKBuilder Terms and Conditions of Participation**

**Effective Date:** April 2026  
**Document Version:** 1.0

This Terms and Conditions document (“Agreement”) governs the obligations, expectations, and entitlements of participants (“Participant”) in the **CKBuilder** track under the Community Keeps Building initiative, managed by the Nervos Community Catalyst. The CKBuilder track is designed to help developers new to blockchain development upscale their skills through structured learning deliverables.

Please refer to the [programme introduction](https://talk.nervos.org/t/announcing-the-launch-of-the-nervos-community-catalyst/8759) for general information about Community Keeps Building.

By enrolling in the CKBuilder track, the Participant agrees to the following terms:

---

## **1\. Scope of Work**

### **1.1 Track Participants**

Participants in the Builder track will be provided with:

* A defined set of learning responsibilities

* A regular output schedule (typically monthly)

* Performance expectations tied to course completion

All work must support the initiative’s goals of developer education, skills certification, and ecosystem growth.

### **1.2 Specific Deliverables and Remuneration**

**Participant:** \[Name\]  
 **Selected Track:** Builder  
 **Term of Agreement:** 3 months (subject to extension)

**Expected Outputs:**

* **Course Completion:**

  * Over the course of 3 months, complete the following course learning materials and deliverables:  
    * [**CKB Academy**](https://academy.ckb.dev/courses) \- 8 modules to read, watch, and interact with. Plus an interactive tutorial  
      * One of the modules is the L1 developer training course. This uses Lumos, a now outdated framework, but many of the lessons and tutorials will be useful for your general learning of CKB  
    * [**CCC Playground**](https://docs.ckbccc.com/docs/CCC) \- test your code and complete the examples on the CCC interactive playground  
    * Set up your own CKB node and developer environment  
    * All of the above and additional learning materials are now available in the [**CKBuilders Handbook**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aFHXU1ZL1MyIbBAIVRjG6stqdWwPUPyHV90O1QNwY-M/edit?usp=sharing)  
* **Progress Reporting:**

  * Submit a brief weekly status update (via template) outlining courses completed, scores achieved, and key learnings. Please note that the report should be an entry in your CKBuilder github repository and should be provided once per week, not aggregated over a few weeks or at the end of the month. An aggregated report will count as only one report.

* **Capstone Submission:**

  * **In month 3**, use what you have learned to create a small application and showcase your skills.

**Remuneration:**

* **Monthly Stipend:** \$300 USD equivalent paid in CKB, upon approval of that month’s deliverables. Payment is proportional to the number of weekly reports received in that calendar month.

**Review Frequency & Reviewer:**

* Monthly reviews by the Community Catalyst lead (e.g., **Neon**) to confirm completion and approve stipend.

## **2\. Expectations**

### **2.1 General Conduct**

Participants must:

* Act professionally and in good faith.

* Ensure originality—avoid AI-generated or plagiarised content.

### **2.2 Track Activity**

* Maintain timely communication on progress and blockers.

* Submit all deliverables according to the agreed schedule.

* Participate in any feedback sessions when requested.

## **3\. Appraisal and Review**

* Operates on a monthly cycle.

* Participants submit proof of their work or learning.

* Self-report forms may be required.

* Feedback or requests for revision must be addressed within 5 business days.

* Approval of deliverables determines stipend eligibility and continuation.

## **4\. Payments**

### **4.1 Structure**

* All payments are denominated in CKB at a USD-equivalent rate determined on payment date.

### **4.2 Track Payments**

* Monthly stipends assume full deliverable completion and review approval.

* Partial completion may merit prorated payment at the coordinator’s discretion.

### **4.3 Payment Processing**

* Issued between the 10th-12th of the subsequent month.

* Delays may occur due to review or blockchain network conditions.

## **5\. Limitation of Liability**

The program organizers shall not be liable for:

* Indirect or consequential damages from participation.

* Platform outages or policy changes affecting deliverable submission.

* Lost or misdirected payments due to incorrect wallet details (Participants are responsible for accuracy).

## **6\. Termination**

### **6.1 Grounds for Termination**

* Repeated failure to meet deliverables.

* Misconduct, plagiarism, or public misrepresentation.

* Prolonged inactivity or communication breakdown.

Termination may forfeit eligibility for future funding.

### **6.2 Voluntary Withdrawal**

Participants may withdraw at any time in writing. Approved deliverables up to withdrawal date will be remunerated.

## **7\. Governance and Revisions**

These terms may be updated as the initiative transitions toward Community DAO governance. Material changes will be communicated in advance.

**8\. Contact**

For questions or appeals, reach out to the programme lead via official channels (e.g., Telegram, email).
